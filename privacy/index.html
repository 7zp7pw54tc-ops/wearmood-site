1. Who we are

Wearmood: AI Stylist ("Wearmood", "we", "us") is an iPhone app for organising the clothes you own. It is operated by Rocaret LLC, a New York limited liability company, 418 Broadway, Ste N, Albany, NY 12207, United States, which is responsible for your personal data under this policy. You can reach us at support@wearmood.app.

2. What we collect

Your account

  • Your email address and how you sign in (Apple, Google, or email and password).
  • From Sign in with Apple or Google: an account identifier and an email address. With Apple, you can hide your email and we receive a private relay address. We don't ask either for your name.
  • If you use email, your password is stored only as a secure hash by our database provider. We never see it.

Your profile

  • Your username (used for greetings and your account; it isn't shown to other people).
  • Your gender, if you choose one. "Prefer not to say" is always an option, and a self-described answer is stored as you type it. We use gender only to personalise defaults, such as which categories come first.
  • Your time zone and app settings (for example, whether review reminders are on).

Your age range

  • When you create an account, you choose an age range. If it's 18 or over, we store that adult range with your account, and when you confirmed it.
  • We never ask for or store your date of birth.
  • If you choose an under-18 range, nothing about it leaves your phone. The app only keeps a note on your device that the check didn't pass.

Your wardrobe photos, in every version

  • The original photo you take or pick.
  • The versions made from it: a cutout and a cleaned-up photo (made on your phone), an enhanced photo and any Retry results (made by an AI service, only when you tap), and small thumbnails.
  • When you tap Enhance before saving a new item, its photos are uploaded first so the enhanced version can be made.

Photos can show your home, your body or other people, so we suggest photographing clothes on a plain surface (see our Terms).

Details about your items

  • Tags: category, type, colours, pattern, material, formality and seasons, with how confident the tagging was.
  • The item's name and brand, if tagging suggests them or you type them.
  • Which tags you corrected or confirmed, whether an item is archived, and which photo version you chose to show.

Your AI choice

Whether you chose Allow or Not now on the AI screen, and when.

Usage counts

  • How many photos you've enhanced and how many Retry tries you've used each month, so we can apply the limits of your plan.
  • A record of each AI request (which feature, which provider, its size, its cost, whether it worked, and when), so we can enforce rate limits and control costs.

Anonymous analytics

The app sends anonymous usage events to TelemetryDeck, such as "item added" or "enhance tapped", with the app version, iOS version and device type. Each install gets a random identifier that is hashed on your phone before it's sent, so it can't be linked to your account. Analytics never contain your photos, username, gender, email or age range.

Crash reports

When the app crashes or hits an error, it sends a report to Sentry: what went wrong in the code, the device model, iOS and app version, and an anonymous install identifier. Reports never include screenshots, your photos, your account details or what you typed.

Approximate location (only if you turn it on)

Location is off until you turn it on, from the Today screen or in Settings › Weather; the app asks iOS for permission only then, and only while you're using the app. We take an approximate location from your phone (never your precise location), round it on your phone to about 11 km (0.1 degrees), and store only that rounded position with your account. We use it only to get the weather for your daily outfits: our server sends that approximate position and the date to Apple's WeatherKit service, with no account details or photos. While location is on, the app checks it at most once a day when you open it, and saves a new position only if you've moved more than about 10 km. It never tracks you in the background.

You can turn location off at any time in Settings › Weather, which deletes the stored position straight away. If you take away Wearmood's location permission in iOS Settings, we delete it the next time you open the app.

Daily outfits and your reactions

Each day we suggest outfits made only from your own wardrobe, by fixed rules on our server (no AI). We store the outfits we suggest, with the weather they were made for, and your reactions, including any note you add, and looks you favorite: which looks you open, favorite or dislike (with the reasons you pick), and the look you choose to wear that day. A note (up to 140 characters, only with the "Other" reason) is optional. We use all of this only to make your outfits better; your reactions, notes and favorites are never sent to analytics, advertisers or AI providers. When daily reminders arrive, we'll also store a notification token and update this policy first.

What we don't collect

We don't collect your date of birth, contacts, precise location, advertising identifier, browsing history or payment card details. In-app purchases, when offered, are handled by Apple; we only receive the state of your subscription.

4. Your photos and AI

Before you add your first photo, the app asks whether it may use AI for your photos. Nothing is pre-selected.

  • Allow: each new photo's cleaned-up version is sent to Anthropic to fill in its tags. A photo goes to OpenAI only when you tap Enhance (or Enhance all), and to Google only when you tap Retry. Nothing is enhanced automatically.
  • Not now: no photo is sent to any AI provider. You add tags yourself, and photos stay as cleaned up on your phone.
  • You can change your answer at any time in Settings › AI. Turning AI off stops new photos being sent; it doesn't delete photos already in your wardrobe.
  • On every item you can show the original or the cleaned-up photo instead of the enhanced one.

No training. These providers process your photos on our behalf under their business and data processing terms, and none of them may use your photos to train or improve their AI models.

How long providers keep them. Providers may keep a request for a limited period to detect misuse, as their terms allow (OpenAI up to 30 days), and then delete it. For Enhance all, photos wait in OpenAI's batch storage until the results come back (within 24 hours), and we delete those files once we have the results.

AI can be wrong. Tags and enhanced photos are generated automatically. Every enhanced photo is checked against your cleaned-up photo for changes in colour, texture and shape, and your original is always kept.

6. What we never do

  • We don't sell your personal data, or share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.
  • We don't show your photos or wardrobe to other users, advertisers or analytics services.
  • The app shows no ads. If we ever add ads, we'll update this policy first, and we'll never pass your photos, gender, username, age range or style data to an ad network.

We disclose data only to the providers above, when the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or to a successor if Wearmood is sold or merged (this policy would still apply to your data).

7. How long we keep it

  • Your account, profile and wardrobe: until you delete them.
  • An item you delete: its details and every version of its photo (original, cutout, cleaned-up, enhanced, Retry results and thumbnails) are deleted straight away. Archived items are kept until you delete them.
  • Photos for an item you never saved: files uploaded for an Enhance on the review screen, or left by an interrupted upload, are deleted automatically within about two days.
  • Retry results you didn't choose: deleted after 24 hours.
  • Your approximate location: until you turn location off in Settings › Weather (deleted straight away), take away Wearmood's location permission in iOS (deleted the next time you open the app) or delete your account.
  • Suggested outfits, your reactions, including any note you add, and looks you favorite: kept with your account until you delete it (taking back a favorite removes it from your favorites). If you delete an item, past outfits keep a "Removed item" in its place, with no photo.
  • AI request records: kept with your account; when you delete your account they're kept only as anonymous cost records (see below).
  • Crash reports: kept by Sentry for up to 90 days.
  • Anonymous analytics: kept by TelemetryDeck as anonymous statistics; they can't be linked to you.

8. Deleting your account

Open Settings › Account › Delete account, confirm it's you (with Apple, Google or your password) and type DELETE. This happens immediately, in the same request:

  • Deleted: your wardrobe items and every photo version, tags, names and brands, your profile (username, gender, time zone, approximate location), your age range, your AI choice, your usage counts, your suggested outfits, your reactions, including any note you add, and looks you favorite, and your sign-in account.
  • If you signed in with Apple, we revoke Wearmood's access to your Apple account. If you used Google, you can also remove Wearmood from your Google Account's third-party connections.
  • A file still being written when you deleted your account is removed by our nightly cleanup within about 24 hours. Photos already sent in an Enhance all batch are deleted from OpenAI when that batch ends, within about 49 hours.
  • We keep anonymous cost records (feature, provider, size, cost, status and time), with no link to you, for our accounts.
  • If our database provider keeps backups, deleted data leaves them within 7 days.

Can't open the app? Email support@wearmood.app from the address on your account and we'll delete it for you.

9. Adults only

Wearmood is only for people aged 18 or over. We don't knowingly collect data from anyone under 18. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, email us and we'll close it and delete its data.

10. Your rights

Wherever you live, you can:

  • See and correct your data: most of it is in the app, where you can edit your profile and tags.
  • Get a copy of your data in a portable format.
  • Delete items, or your whole account, in the app.
  • Withdraw consent to AI processing in Settings › AI, without affecting processing that already happened.
  • Object to or restrict processing based on our legitimate interests.

To use a right that isn't in the app, email support@wearmood.app from the address on your account. We'll reply within one month. We may need to confirm it's you first. You won't be treated differently for using your rights.

EEA, UK and Switzerland: you can also complain to your local data protection authority.

US state residents (including California, Colorado, Connecticut, Virginia and others): you have the rights to know, access, correct and delete your personal data, and to opt out of its sale or sharing for targeted advertising. We don't sell or share it. You can use an authorised agent, and you can appeal a decision by replying to our answer.

11. International transfers

Our providers process data in the United States and other countries where they operate. Where data leaves the EEA, UK or Switzerland, the transfer is covered by the providers' data processing terms, including the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where applicable.

12. Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Each account can read only its own data, enforced in the database. Photos are kept in private storage and shown only through short-lived links. Sign-in sessions are stored in your iPhone's Keychain. AI service keys never leave our servers. No system is perfectly secure; if a breach affects your data, we'll tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

13. This website

wearmood.app uses no cookies, analytics or trackers. Our web host processes visitors' IP addresses to deliver the pages and keep them secure.

14. Changes

We'll update this policy when the app changes, for example before daily outfits or subscriptions ship, and change the date at the top. If a change matters, we'll tell you in the app before it applies.
