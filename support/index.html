Questions, problems or data requests: write to us and a person will reply, usually within two working days.

support@wearmood.app

For anything about your account, write from the email address you signed up with, so we can find it.

Common questions

How do I delete my account?

In the app, open Settings › Account › Delete account, confirm it's you and type DELETE. Everything is deleted straight away (see the Privacy Policy). If you can't open the app, email us from your account's address.

How do I stop sending photos to AI?

Settings › AI › turn off "Use AI for photos". You can add tags yourself, and photos stay as cleaned up on your phone.

An enhanced photo doesn't look like my item.

Open the item and choose "Use cleaned-up photo" or "Use original photo". Your original is always kept.

I forgot my password.

On the sign-in screen, tap "Forgot password?" and we'll email you a reset link. Check your spam folder if it doesn't arrive within a few minutes.

How do I get a copy of my data?

Email us from your account's address and we'll send it within one month.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Wearmood is free during the beta. When subscriptions are offered, they're managed by Apple: iPhone Settings › your name › Subscriptions.
