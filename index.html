Your own closet, styled every morning.

Wearmood: AI Stylist turns the clothes you already own into a tidy virtual wardrobe, and soon into a daily outfit that matches your style.

  • A wardrobe that looks like a shopPhotograph your clothes and Wearmood cuts them out and evens the light on your phone, so every item sits on the same clean background.
  • Sorted for youEach photo is tagged with its category, colours, material and season, and you can fix any tag in one tap.
  • Enhance when you want itTap Enhance to have AI smooth out wrinkles and hangers. It never runs on its own, and your original photo is always kept.
  • Outfits from your own clothesComing next: a daily outfit built only from what you own, shown next to the look that inspired it.

Private by default

Your wardrobe photos are seen only by you. AI services process them only if you allow it, and they don't use them to train their models. You can delete your account and everything in it from the app at any time.
